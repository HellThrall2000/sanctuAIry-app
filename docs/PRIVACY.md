# Privacy Policy — sanctuAIry

**Last updated: 3 August 2026**

sanctuAIry is a private journalling and self-reflection app with an AI companion
that runs on your phone. This policy explains exactly what we do and do not
collect.

## The short version

**Nothing you write is ever sent anywhere.** Your conversations with the
companion, your diary entries, the memories it forms about you, your moods and
your topics stay in storage on your phone. There is no server that holds them. We
could not read them if we wanted to, and neither could anyone who compromised us.

What we do collect is a count of how much the app is used, so we know whether
anyone is using it.

---

## What stays on your device, always

- Every message you send the companion, and every reply
- Every diary entry, including locked ones
- Everything the companion remembers about you — facts, moods, topics, events,
  session summaries
- Your passcode
- The AI model itself, once downloaded

None of this is uploaded, backed up to us, or transmitted anywhere. Deleting the
app deletes all of it.

**The companion runs on your phone.** Your messages are not sent to OpenAI,
Google, Anthropic or any other service for processing. This is why the app has to
download a large model file once — it is the reason the app can make this promise
at all.

---

## What we collect

For each day you use the app, we record:

- How long the app was open that day, in hours
- How many times you opened it
- How many messages you sent — **the number, never the content**
- How many diary entries you wrote — **the number, never the content**
- Whether the model finished downloading and loaded successfully
- Your app version and platform

That is a set of numbers against a date. Nothing in it describes what you did,
only how much.

### Accounts

**On Android, signing in with Google is required to use the app.** We store the
**email address** and **display name** on your Google account, and nothing else
from it — no contacts, no calendar, no Drive, no profile details beyond your
name.

**On iOS, signing in is optional.** If you skip it, the app uses an anonymous
account: a random identifier not linked to your name, email, phone number or
device.

Nothing in the app is unlocked or improved by an account. It exists so we can
tell how many people use sanctuAIry.

### Working offline

Usage is recorded on your device and sent when you next have a connection, so
using the app offline still counts. Once a day's figures reach our server, the
local copy is deleted from your phone.

### Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics for aggregate figures such as daily active users.
Google may also process device and general-location information as part of that
service; see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We
send Analytics no personal information and no content.

---

## What we never collect

To be unambiguous, we do not collect, transmit or store:

- The text of your messages, or the companion's replies
- The text or titles of your diary entries
- Anything the companion has remembered about you
- Your mood, sentiment or emotional state
- Your health information
- Your contacts, photos, files, precise location, or microphone

The app requests no permission that would allow it to. Its only network use is
downloading the model once, and signing in if you ask it to.

---

## Why we collect anything at all

To know whether the app is worth continuing to build, and to see whether it works
in practice — for instance, whether the first-run download tends to fail on real
phones. That is the whole purpose. We do not sell data, we do not share it with
advertisers, and there is no advertising in the app.

---

## Where it is held

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, operated by Google. Data in transit
is encrypted. Access is restricted so that each account can reach only its own
record, and no client can read anyone else's.

We keep usage records for as long as the account exists.

## Your choices

| You want to | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Use the app without an account | On iOS, choose "Continue without an account" on the first screen. Not available on Android |
| Delete your account and its usage record | **Settings → your profile → Delete account.** Immediate and permanent |
| Delete what the companion knows about you | **Settings → Companion Memory → Review & Forget** |
| Delete everything | Uninstall the app. Everything on the device goes with it |

If you are in a jurisdiction with rights of access, correction, portability,
erasure or objection — the UK and EU under UK GDPR and GDPR, and others — you
can exercise them by writing to the address below. In practice the account
deletion above already performs an erasure.

## Children

sanctuAIry is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.

---

## This is not a medical service

sanctuAIry is a journalling and reflection companion. It is not therapy, it does
not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a crisis
service. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number or a
crisis line.

# Delete your sanctuAIry account

> **Publish this at a public URL** alongside the privacy policy and paste it into
> Play Console → *App content → Data safety → Data deletion*. Google Play requires
> a web page where users can ask for their account to be deleted *without*
> reinstalling the app. Replace the contact address before publishing.

## In the app

**Settings → your profile → Delete account.**

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes:

- your sanctuAIry account (the Google email and display name we stored)
- every daily usage record attached to it

## Without the app

If you have uninstalled sanctuAIry or cannot open it, email
**REPLACE_ME@example.com** from the Google account you signed in with, with the
subject **"Delete my sanctuAIry account"**. We delete the account and its usage
records within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What there is to delete

Very little is held off your phone at all. See the
[privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for the full list.

| Data | Where | Deleted by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email, display name, account id | Our server | Account deletion |
| Daily usage counts | Our server | Account deletion |
| Conversations, diary, memories, moods | **Your phone only** | Uninstalling the app |
| Replies you chose to report | Our server, with nothing identifying you | Not linked to your account, so there is nothing to match them to |

Nothing is kept after deletion, and there is no retention period.
